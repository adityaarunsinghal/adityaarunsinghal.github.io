# Privacy

Source: https://adityasinghal.com/privacy/

About this website



How this website handles visits, analytics and embedded content.

This notice covers Aditya Singhal's personal website at `adityasinghal.com` and workshop pages at `learningwithagents.com`. Public pages and content downloads can be read without signing in. This page describes the services used across these sites and the choices available in your browser.

Last reviewed November 18, 2026.

## Hosting and request data

GitHub Pages hosts these sites. Requests reach GitHub's infrastructure, which may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and request time to deliver and protect the service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for its handling of that information.

## Public-page analytics

The personal homepage and the 2026 and 2025 workshop pages use the same Google Analytics 4 property to understand visits and use of public resources. Reports can distinguish visits by domain. Google's tag uses cookies and processes usage and device information. Google signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled.

The sites' custom events record page views and selected resource, social-link and video interactions. Their page address uses the domain serving the page and a normalized local path, without query strings or fragments; their referrer is reduced to its origin. A workshop's search-engine canonical address does not change its analytics domain. These custom events do not include chapter-search text, email addresses, complete outbound URLs or private application content.

The developer guide, this privacy page and public JSON or Markdown files do not load the analytics tag. You can restrict cookies or block analytics requests through your browser. Google also provides an [Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information about its services.

## Fonts and video

The personal homepage requests its font stylesheet and font files from Google Fonts. This sends technical request information to Google. Workshop fonts are served from the site displaying the workshop. See [Google Fonts' privacy information](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

Personal-video previews on the personal homepage are stored on that site. A YouTube player loads after you choose a video. Workshop recordings use lazy-loaded YouTube players, which can contact YouTube as they approach the visible part of the page, before playback.

The embedded players use YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, `youtube-nocookie.com`. Loading or playing a video still involves requests to YouTube and related services. See [YouTube's explanation of privacy-enhanced embeds](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780). You can follow the ordinary video links to watch on YouTube instead.

## Forms, messages and other websites

Workshop forms open Google Forms, and information submitted there goes to the form owner. Social profiles, code repositories, publications and book-purchase links open their respective services. Their privacy practices apply when you visit, sign in or submit information there.

Choosing a contact link does not send a message on your behalf. You decide what to share in the destination service.

## Website assistant

The personal homepage, developer guide and this page load a chat assistant hosted by Ambipath, and AI agents can ask it public questions directly. Where a workshop page offers the assistant, it uses the same assistant and the same data handling. Your browser connects to Ambipath to load it. Messages you send it, and any contact details you choose to share, go to Ambipath and are visible to me in its console. See [Ambipath's privacy notice](https://console.ambipath.com/privacy).

## Signed-in utilities and browser storage

Separate private utilities on the personal website use Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication and store authorized application data in Firebase Firestore. Authentication information and application data are used to provide access to those utilities. The public profile and workshop pages do not require this sign-in.

Firebase can keep sign-in state and cached application data in your browser. Sign out after use on a shared device; browser settings can also clear this site's stored data. Clearing browser storage does not by itself delete data stored by a service.
