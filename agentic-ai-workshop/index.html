HOW WE LEARN

Keep your laptop closed and ask questions.

Watch the result in class and predict the next step. We’ll inspect the mechanism together.

Build between sessions using slides and starter code. You’ll be encouraged to share your agent through a common class app store.

IN THE ROOM

60 minutes of material + 30 minutes of Q&A

Technical storytelling through slides, live demonstrations, and conversation.

BETWEEN SESSIONS

About 1–2 hours of development each week

Choose a useful task for your project. As you extend it, bring what you learned back to class.

SUPPORT

Friday office hours

Personalized help and model inference through OpenRouter for registered students. Access and joining details will be shared with the class.

Before you join

Who is the workshop for?

Members of the CDS community, including students and alumni. Undergraduates should have completed DS-UA 112. Attend the first three sessions and plan for weekly project work.

Where do we meet?

CDS 7th Floor Open Space, 60 5th Ave, between 12th and 13th Streets, New York City. Thursdays, 5:30–7:00 PM ET.

The workshop is in person. Contact the organizer about individual attendance questions.

What will I need?

Time to build between sessions and curiosity in the room. Slides and starter code are coming soon. Registered students will receive inference access through OpenRouter.

How do I register or ask a question?

Register by Monday, October 5 at 5 PM ET. The registration form requires Google sign-in.

Contact the workshop organizer about form access, attendance, or other questions.
